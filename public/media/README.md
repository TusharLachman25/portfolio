# Screenshots and videos go here

Every slot on the site already renders a labelled placeholder telling you
which filename it wants. **Drop a file in with that exact name and it
appears — there is no code to change.**

## Before you publish — read this

Three of these screens contain **real private data**. Do not publish them as-is:

- **Jarvis** shows your actual bank balances, tuition, remittances, Gmail and health data. Seed it with fake numbers before capturing anything.
- **The medical platform** contains patient information (PHI) and Dr Varkha's registration numbers, signature seal, phone and bank details. Ask her permission first, use invented demo patients, and blur or replace her credentials and account numbers.
- **Locked In** shows your friends' names, photos and profiles. Ask them, or capture with test accounts.

Never publish a real patient record, even redacted.

## Practical tips

- **Aspect ratio**: shots render at 16:10, video at 16:9. Anything close is fine — they're cropped to fit.
- **Size**: aim for ~1600px wide PNGs. Compress with [squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) so the page stays fast.
- **Phone screens**: Locked In is a portrait app. Either screenshot the web build, or place two phone screens side by side on a dark background so the image fills the 16:10 frame instead of leaving big empty margins.
- **Recording**: Windows `Win + Alt + R`, or [ScreenToGif](https://www.screentogif.com) for something lighter. Keep it under 90 seconds, no narration needed, and show the interesting part in the first 10 seconds.
